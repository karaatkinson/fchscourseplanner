# **French I**

| Course Title | French I |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | World Languages |
| **Course Description** | Most instruction will be in the target language. This course encourages interpersonal communication through speaking and writing in the language, providing opportunities to exchange information with others on familiar topics, understand and use appropriate greetings and forms of address, and write short passages with guidance. # **French II**

| Course Title | French II |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | World Languages |
| **Course Description** | Most instruction will be in the target language. A sound understanding of vocabulary and grammatical structures from the first-year level is required. This course encourages interpersonal communication through speaking and writing, providing opportunities to make and respond to requests and questions in familiar situations, participate independently in brief conversations on familiar topics, and write with greater independence.
