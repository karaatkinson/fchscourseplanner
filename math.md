| Course Title | Calculus Dual Credit |
| :---- | :---- |

However, it’s not guaranteed that you will be able to transfer the college credits you earn to your targeted 4-year university. Do some research. AP courses could be the edge you need to get into a competitive university or be awarded scholarships. The student who takes this course should not be planning a career that is heavy science/math based. This course would be perfect, for example, for the liberal arts majors out there.
