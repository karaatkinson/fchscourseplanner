# **Career Exploration Internship**

| Course Title | Career Exploration Internship |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | Multidisciplinary |
| **Course Description** | Career Exploration Internship is a paid or unpaid work experience, in the public or private sector, in an area of the student's career interest. Rather than training for one specific job, the internship exposes students to broad aspects of an industry or career area, ideally by rotating through different work sites or departments. Students taking the course for one class period work 5 hours per week; students taking it for two class periods work 10 hours per week. In addition to their work hours, students meet with the teacher every Monday to connect what they learn in school to their work experience. The student, parent/guardian, employer, and teacher agree on a written training plan with learning goals tied to the student's career pathway, so everyone knows what is expected. |

# **Work Based Learning Capstone**

| Course Title | Work Based Learning Capstone |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | Multidisciplinary |
| **Course Description** | Work Based Learning Capstone is a stand-alone course that prepares students for college and career. Students will work in a real workplace setting related to a career matched with their CTE Pathway. They will spend one day a week in the classroom seventh period for instruction and other days they will be dismissed to complete their "on-the-job" duties. Some students will go directly to work and others will be dismissed early, due to the fact they work outside of the usual school day at their employer. The purposes of the program are to: help students identify their strengths and weaknesses, give students an opportunity to learn by experience not available to them in the classroom, and give students guidance on opportunities available to them in the future. Students will be required to remain employed the entire time they are in the course. The job must be meaningful and related to their future career interest. Their job will be part of their grade and they must work a minimum of five hours per week, hours must be submitted. Part of their grade will be based on their classwork and part of their grade will be based on their work performance. The related instruction will contain both employability skills and specific job-related skills. Students must successfully complete the application process to be admitted to the course. |
| **Grade Level(s)** | 12 |
| **Length & Credits** | 2 semesters / 2 credits |
