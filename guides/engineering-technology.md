# **Principles of Construction Trades**

| Course Title | Principles of Construction Trades |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | Engineering and Technology Education |
| **Course Description** | Principles of Construction Trades prepares students with the basic skills needed to continue in a construction trade field. Topics will include an introduction to the types and uses for common hand and power tools, learn the types and basic terminology associated with construction drawings, and basic safety. # **Introduction to Engineering Design**

| Course Title | Introduction to Engineering Design |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | Engineering and Technology Education |
| **Course Description** | Have you wondered what an engineer does for a career? Do you like drawing and designing? Have you thought about how a product works? Do you like to solve problems? Do you like to build models? Are you interested in learning technical drawing and computer modeling? If so, Introduction to Engineering Design is the class for you. This course is an introduction to problem-solving with an emphasis on the development of three-dimensional computer models. # **Principles of Engineering**

| Course Title | Principles of Engineering |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | Engineering and Technology Education |
| **Course Description** | Do you want to learn more about the engineering field? Want to explore the mechanical features of machines? Do you have an interest in robotics? Have you wondered how bridges are designed? Want to expand your knowledge and apply concepts of math and science? Want to complete an engineering pathway? If so, Principles of Engineering is the class for you\! Principles of Engineering is a course that focuses on the process of applying engineering, technological, scientific, and mathematical principles in the design, production, and operation of products, structures, and systems. This is a hands-on course designed to provide students interested in engineering careers to explore experiences related to specialized fields such as civil, mechanical, and materials engineering. Students will engage in research, development, planning, design, production, and project management to simulate a career in engineering. The topics of ethics and the impacts of engineering decisions are also addressed. Classroom activities are organized to allow students to work in teams and use modern technological processes, computers, CAD software, and production systems in developing and presenting solutions to engineering problems. | :---- | :---- |
| **Department** | Engineering and Technology Education |
| **Course Description** | Want to design your DREAM home? Ever wonder what a civil engineer or architect does for a living?
