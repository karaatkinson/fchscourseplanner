How this site works

  • Course Catalog | browse by department, search, or filter (AP, dual credit, pathway, and more). Click a course to see full details.
  • My Schedule | click "Add to Schedule" on any course. It lands in Semester 1 or 2 automatically; toggle S1/S2 to move it. More than 7 in a semester goes to Alternatives.
  • Year tabs | Junior High through 12th Grade, each with its own schedule. Mark past years as completed to log real credit.
  • Indiana Diploma Progress | see how your planned courses add up against graduation requirements and readiness seals. Click any requirement to see exactly which courses count toward it.
  • Indiana College Core | tracks dual-credit and AP progress toward the 30-credit college certificate.
