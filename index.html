How to use this guide

  • Departments | pick a department on the left (or across the top on a phone), or choose All Departments.
  • Search | type a course name, course code, or any word from a description.
  • Filters | narrow the list by grade level, AP, dual credit, NCAA approval, and more.
  • Pathways | under Filters, choose a pathway to see its courses in the order to take them. When a step lists more than one course, choose one.
  • Course details | click any course to see its full description, prerequisites, credits, and dual-credit information. Underlined course names (in prerequisites and pathway lists) take you to that course.
  • Share a course | the address bar updates when you open a course, so you can copy and send the link.
